A print is half the job. The frame, the mat, and the height you hang it at decide whether it
reads as *art* or as *paper on a wall* — and that part costs less and matters more than most
people expect.

These guides cover the practical side: which frame size fits which print, when a mat is worth
it, how to hang without putting holes in a rented wall, and how to space a gallery wall so it
looks deliberate. Our prints ship unframed, so this is the missing half of the instructions.
