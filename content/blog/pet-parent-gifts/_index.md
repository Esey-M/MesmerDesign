Pet people are the easiest group to buy for and the easiest to get wrong. Easy, because the
subject is obvious. Wrong, because they already own eleven squeaky toys.

The gifts that land are either genuinely practical — the hair remover they resent buying — or
genuinely sentimental, like a portrait of the animal itself. These guides cover both.
