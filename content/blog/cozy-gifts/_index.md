Comfort is the one category almost nobody buys enough of for themselves. People replace a phone
without blinking and keep the same thin blanket for nine years.

That's what makes it such reliable gifting ground. These guides cover the warm, slow, tactile
things — blankets, candles, thick socks, a mug that stays hot — for the person who insists they
don't need anything.
