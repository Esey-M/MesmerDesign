## Why "bold and easy" is the whole point

The intricate adult coloring books that sold by the million a decade ago had a problem nobody
said out loud: they were *work*. Three hundred hair-thin petals, a fineliner, and a growing
sense that you were failing at relaxation.

Bold and easy is the correction. Thick outlines you can follow at a glance. Shapes big enough
to fill in one confident stroke. Every line closed, so a marker stays where you put it. You
can finish a page in one evening instead of abandoning it in March — and finishing is the part
that actually feels good.
