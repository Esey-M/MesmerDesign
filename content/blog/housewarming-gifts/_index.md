A new home has a very specific gap: everything structural is handled and none of the small
things are. They have a sofa and no coasters, a kitchen and no decent serving board.

That's the sweet spot for a housewarming gift — and the same logic covers a hostess gift, where
the goal is something she can use *that evening* rather than store.
