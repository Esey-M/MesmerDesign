{{< answer >}}
**Bring the small practical thing they haven't got round to buying** — coasters, a serving board, good kitchen towels. New homes have the furniture handled and the small stuff missing. And bring the pot, not the plant: a plant is a commitment they didn't choose.
{{< /answer >}}

Someone moving into a new place has spent months thinking about sofas and nothing at all about coasters. Then they sit down with a coffee on a brand-new table and realise.

That gap is the entire housewarming gift category.

## The things every new home is missing

{{< amazon-grid ids="serving-board, coaster-set, kitchen-towel-set" >}}

The **serving board** is the classic for a reason — it gets used the first time they have people over, which for a new home is usually within a fortnight. Acacia is the sensible default: dense, moisture-resistant, and it looks better than it costs.

Coasters are the unglamorous winner. Nobody buys their own until after the first ring appears on the table.

## For the evening itself

{{< amazon-grid ids="candle-gift-set, candle-lighter, indoor-plant-pot" >}}

A candle plus a rechargeable lighter is a better pairing than either alone — the lighter outlives every box of damp matches in the kitchen drawer.

And on plants: **bring the pot, not the plant.** A plant is a living obligation they didn't agree to. A good pot is a gift they get to decide about.
