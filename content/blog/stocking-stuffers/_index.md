Stockings are where good intentions go to die. Bought in a hurry on the 23rd, opened in ninety
seconds, and quietly binned by February.

The fix is a low bar, honestly applied: will they still have it in March? These guides collect
small gifts that pass that test — consumable, pocket-sized, or genuinely useful.
