## Built to feel like play

Handwriting sticks for little kids when it doesn't feel like a chore. This workbook is designed
to feel like a game: kids warm up with wiggly stroke drills, trace friendly shapes (circle,
square, triangle, heart), then meet every capital and lowercase letter on big, clear three-line
guides. They count stars as they learn their numbers, write their very first words — including
their own name — and color a star on the reward chart after each finished page.

The stars build toward a printable Certificate of Awesome Handwriting at the very end, which is
exactly the kind of finish line a five-year-old will work for. And because there's a short note
for grown-ups up front with pencil-grip and pacing tips, you don't need a teaching background —
just ten minutes and a spot at the kitchen table.
