Every printable we make is a PDF. What it becomes depends entirely on what you print it on and
what you do with it afterwards — and the difference between copy paper and 110 lb cardstock is
the difference between a sheet that curls and one that feels like a card you'd keep.

These guides cover the boring, useful part: which paper weight for which pack, whether you need
a laminator or just a dry-erase pocket, and the small kit that makes a handwriting or activity
pack reusable instead of single-use.
