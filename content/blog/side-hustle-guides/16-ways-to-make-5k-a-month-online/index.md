## The problem with every other guide

Most "make money online" content has a shape you'll recognise: a long list of ideas, a lot of
encouragement, and not a single number. Start a YouTube channel. Try print on demand. Get into
freelancing. Good luck.

What's missing is always the same three things — what it pays, how long it takes, and what goes
wrong. Without those, you can't compare anything. You pick whichever option had the most
convincing thumbnail, work at it for six weeks, and quit when the results don't match a
timeline nobody ever told you.

This guide is built the other way round. Every path gets the arithmetic first.

## Beyond the list

The back half is the part that makes the list usable, because sixteen options is too many to
choose from:

- A **chart of how many months** each path takes to reach a first $1,000
- A **framework that collapses all 16 into 4 engines** — so you're choosing between four things, not sixteen
- **Three realistic income stacks** showing how people actually assemble $5,000 from more than one source
- A **90-day action plan** you can start today
- **Seven failure patterns** and how to avoid each one
- **What changes between now and 2030**

The stacks matter more than they sound. Almost nobody reaches $5,000 a month from one income
source — they reach it from three that each do a different job, and seeing that laid out ends
a lot of pointless agonising over which single path is "the best one."
