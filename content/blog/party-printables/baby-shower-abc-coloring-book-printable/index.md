## Why guests actually like this one

Most baby shower games ask people to perform. Guess the belly size, smell the nappy, race to
name the celebrity baby — fun for four minutes, awkward for the shy aunt, and in the bin by
Monday.

This one asks nothing of anybody. It sits on the table while people talk, and anyone who wants
something to do with their hands picks up a pencil. Guests who hate games colour a bunny in
peace. Children who came along have a job. And nothing gets thrown away: at the end of the
afternoon you've got 26 pages, each signed by the person who coloured it, that turn into a
real object the parents will read to the baby.

## How to run it on the day

1. **Print one page per guest** — plus a few extras of the easy letters for any children
2. **Set them out with coloured pencils**, not markers — pencils photograph better and won't bleed through
3. **Ask each guest to sign their page** when they finish
4. **Collect and bind** — two hole-punches and a ribbon is genuinely enough, or slide them into a folder

Put the welcome page beside the pens so nobody has to ask what they're meant to do. It explains
the activity in one glance, which saves the host repeating it thirty times.
