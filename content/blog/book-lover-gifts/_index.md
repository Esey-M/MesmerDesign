The problem with buying for a reader is obvious: they've already chosen their books, and they
have opinions. Buy them a novel and you're guessing against someone who tracks their to-read
list.

So this section skips the books. It's about the things around the reading — the light that
doesn't wake a partner, the sleeve that stops a paperback getting wrecked in a bag, the shelf
that finally looks intentional. Things a reader wants but rarely buys for themselves.
