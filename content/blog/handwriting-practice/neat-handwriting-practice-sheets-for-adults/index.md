## Handwriting is a skill, and skills come back

If your handwriting got worse since school, nothing is wrong with you — you just stopped
practicing, and typing took over. This workbook rebuilds print handwriting the way it's actually
rebuilt: slowly, in the right order, with lots of repetition. You start with warm-up stroke
drills (lines, curves, loops), then get a full page for every lowercase and capital letter,
then numbers, punctuation, and the "tricky pairs" people confuse — rn/m, cl/d.

From there it gets practical: 80 themed practice words, grocery lists, work words, card
phrases, days and months, pangrams, and real everyday sentences. Every page is filled edge to
edge, so nothing you print goes to waste. And there's a quiet little system inside to prove
your progress: write one sentence on day one, track thirty days of practice, then write the
same sentence again and compare. That comparison page is the best part of the whole book.
