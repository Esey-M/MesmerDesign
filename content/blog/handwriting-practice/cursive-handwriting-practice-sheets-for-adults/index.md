## Four strokes, then everything connects

Cursive looks complicated until you realize the whole alphabet is built from four simple
strokes — the undercurve, the overcurve, the oval, and the loop. That's where this workbook
begins, as warm-up drills. Then every cursive letter, lowercase and capital, gets a full page
of tracing and independent practice along the guides.

And then comes the step that separates this from a stack of random worksheets: 24 letter-join
drills — in, th, ing, and, double letters and more — because cursive only starts to feel like
cursive once the letters connect. From there you move into whole words, days and months, card
phrases, pangrams, and fully connected sentences. Be patient with r and s; everyone finds them
tricky at first, and the workbook tells you so right on the page. The same quiet 30-day system
is built in: write a sentence on day one, practice daily, write it again on day thirty and
compare.
