The wrapping is the first thing anyone sees and the last thing anyone thinks about. It's also
the cheapest upgrade in gifting: a few dollars of kraft paper and twine makes a modest gift look
deliberate, while a good gift in a crumpled plastic bag reads as an afterthought.

These guides cover the supplies worth keeping in a drawer year-round, and the small habits —
a handwritten card, a real ribbon — that do most of the work.
