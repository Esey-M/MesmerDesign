## Our promise on recommendations

MesmerDesign is the maker of many of the products featured in these guides. When we
recommend one of our own items, it's a product we design and make to order in our own
[Etsy shop](https://mesmerdesing.etsy.com/) — we earn no third-party commission on those
links, because you're buying directly from us.

Some guides also point to products we **don't** make — the frame for a print, the craft
supplies that go with a printable, the book that pairs with a gift. Those are usually
Amazon links, and they work differently.

## Affiliate links

**As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.**

MesmerDesign is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate
advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by
advertising and linking to Amazon.com. If you click an Amazon link in one of our guides
and buy something, we may earn a small commission **at no extra cost to you**.

This never changes what we recommend. We only link to things we'd suggest to a friend, and
we say plainly when a cheaper or better option exists elsewhere. Prices and availability on
Amazon change constantly — the price on Amazon at the time of purchase is the one that
applies.

## About the prices we show

Our guides show **approximate US prices in US dollars**. Our own products are made in the
EU, where listed prices include 20% VAT; most of our buyers are in the US and pay no VAT,
so the price you see at checkout is typically lower. Prices also vary by size, options, and
currency. We show a rounded "under $X" guide price — **Etsy always shows your exact total
at checkout**, so check the listing before ordering.

We aim to give honest, useful guidance — including pointing you to the most affordable
option when that's the right call. If something here isn't helpful, [tell us](/contact/).
